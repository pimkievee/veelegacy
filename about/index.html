Hi, I'm Valerie Adebayo

I know what it's like to build a new chapter from the ground up.

I built a six-figure technology career in Canada without a Canadian degree or prior Canadian work experience. That experience changed how I think about opportunity: access to the right information matters.

I'm a DevOps engineer, a licensed financial professional and the founder of VeeLegacy. My career has taken me through technology, helping people and financial education. I bring those experiences together because finding work and managing money are often connected parts of building the life you want.

When I share a career resource, I want you to know where to search, what to prepare and which questions to ask. When I explain an FHSA, RESP or insurance option, I want you to understand the real-world choices and eligibility rules, not just the terminology. When I recommend a business idea, I want you to know how to begin researching and testing it.

That's why VeeLegacy offers practical digital guides for Canadian job searches (both in Canada and from abroad), settling in Canada, budgeting, buying a first home and exploring side hustles, alongside financial consultations and a personal 60-minute clarity session for career, DevOps, resumes, interviews, business and money conversations.

My goal is simple: to help you find useful resources, make better-informed decisions and move forward with confidence. Not through promises or shortcuts, but through clear examples, experience and a realistic plan.
