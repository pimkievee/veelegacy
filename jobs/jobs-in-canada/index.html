Find legitimate jobs in Canada

A practical guide to searching for legitimate jobs in Canada, checking employer requirements and avoiding recruitment scams.

Start with official sources

Use Canada Job Bank, federal and provincial public-service career portals, and the official career pages of employers. Read the full posting and verify the employer before applying.

Tailor your applications

Match your resume to the actual requirements, highlight measurable achievements and use a clear, readable format. Review every autofilled field before submitting.

Check eligibility

Work authorization, licensing and language requirements vary by role. Do not assume a job offers visa sponsorship unless the employer explicitly confirms it.

Avoid scams

Legitimate job offers should be independently verifiable. Be cautious of upfront payments, requests for banking credentials and interviews conducted only through messaging apps.
