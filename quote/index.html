Why Financial Protection Matters

Life can change quickly. A loss of income, illness, disability, unexpected death, or poor financial planning can place real pressure on the people you care about most.

The right plan can help a family prepare for monthly bills, mortgage or rent, debt, childcare, education expenses, funeral expenses, business obligations, lost income, and estate costs.

Estate and legacy planning

Life Insurance Can Do More Than Most People Realize

Life insurance is not only about replacing income. In the right situation, it can also help create liquidity, support beneficiaries, preserve assets, and make wealth transfer easier for families.

For business owners and high income earners, it may also be part of conversations around succession, estate equalization, corporate planning, wealth transfer, legacy planning, and family protection.

Individual strategies depend on personal circumstances and professional advice. This page avoids promises about tax outcomes or automatic tax reductions.

I understand the real family questions behind protection planning: Would the family have enough income to keep going? Would debt be left behind? Would the children's plans have to change? Would there be financial support or an inheritance to help loved ones move forward?
