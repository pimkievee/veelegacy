Your Journey

Five Simple Steps to Get Started

You can explore the opportunity one step at a time. Here’s what the path looks like:

01

Connect With Me

Let’s talk about the business opportunity, what you’re looking for, and how you can get started.

02

Get Trained

Enrol in the Life Licence Qualification Program (LLQP) through an approved course provider. You can study online and prepare for four licensing exam modules.

03

Get Licensed

Pass the four provincial LLQP exams, then complete the licensing application and applicable sponsorship requirements in your province.

04

Start Helping Families

Once licensed, appointed and trained, begin meeting clients, understanding their needs, and offering suitable protection solutions.

05

Grow Your Business

Build relationships, keep learning, and develop your practice at a pace that fits your goals.

Step 2: Your Training

The Four LLQP Modules

Here is what you will study before taking your provincial exams:

  1. Life Insurance
  2. Accident and Sickness Insurance
  3. Segregated Funds and Annuities
  4. Ethics and Professional Practice
