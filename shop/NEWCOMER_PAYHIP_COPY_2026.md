## Full description
**New country. New opportunities. So many questions.**

Where do you start after arriving in Canada? Which bank should you choose? How do you build Canadian credit? What benefits might your family qualify for? When do Canadian taxes apply—and what should you do in your first 30 days?

**Your First 30 Days in Canada** brings those financial starting points together in one organized guide. Instead of vague instructions to “check online,” you'll find named Canadian providers, eligibility considerations, explanations of common tradeoffs, real-world examples (including Ontario), and clickable links to official sources.

### What you'll explore
- **Your first 30 days:** a practical checklist for important documents, SIN, banking, payments and financial setup.
- **Banking in Canada:** compare no-monthly-fee choices with traditional bank newcomer packages; learn what to ask about fees and eligibility.
- **Building credit:** newcomer cards, secured cards, Canadian credit history, and cash-back versus travel-reward options.
- **Taxes after arrival:** tax residency basics, arrival-year reporting considerations and CRA reference links.
- **Family and government benefits:** understand the application pathways and key eligibility conditions for the Canada Child Benefit and relevant Ontario supports.
- **Savings and your family’s future:** TFSA, FHSA and RESP basics, including when the accounts may be suitable and who can open them.
- **Everyday settling-in finances:** money transfers, budgeting, housing expense questions, common scams and useful official services.
- **Practical resources:** worksheets, a 90-day follow-through plan, worked examples and direct links.
