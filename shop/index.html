Are the guides physical books?

No. These are digital guides. File types and included bonuses are shown on the individual Payhip checkout pages. Nothing is mailed.

What makes the newcomer guide different?

It starts with a first-30-days checklist and connects practical banking, identification, credit, taxes, family benefits and registered accounts with official Canadian links and realistic examples. It is general education, not a substitute for immigration, tax, legal or financial advice.

Is the mortgage calculator included?

The VeeLegacy Canadian mortgage calculator is freely accessible on our website. The First Home Financial Planning Toolkit links to it and provides additional planning resources.
