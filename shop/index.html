First home • Smarter preparation

Your first home could come with tax advantages. Are you using the right tools?

Buying a home is more than saving a down payment. Learn how eligible FHSA contributions can reduce taxable income, how RRSP savings and the Home Buyers’ Plan may help with qualifying home purchases, and how the FHSA and HBP can work together when you meet the rules.

Important: The FHSA and eligible RRSP contributions may provide tax deductions, depending on your circumstances. An HBP withdrawal is not an extra deduction or a guaranteed refund. Your tax outcome depends on your income, available room, timing and eligibility. RESP is an education savings plan, not the home-buying program.
